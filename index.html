When to Visit

Best Time to Visit Kashmir

Kashmir has 4 seasons — every season has its own beauty. Choose the one that suits your mood.

🌷

Spring

March – May

Tulip gardens bloom, blossoms everywhere, pleasant weather — perfect for sightseeing.

☀️

Summer

June – August

Best weather, green meadows, peak tourist season. Great for family holidays.

🍁

Autumn

September – November

Golden Chinar leaves, quieter roads, stunning photography light.

❄️

Winter

December – February

Snow-covered landscapes, skiing in Gulmarg, frozen Dal Lake views.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you plan your Kashmir trip.

Is Kashmir safe for tourists?
Yes, Kashmir is safe for tourists. Millions of travellers visit every year. We provide verified hotels, experienced local drivers, and 24/7 on-ground support throughout your trip.
What is the best time to visit Kashmir?
Kashmir has 4 seasons and every season has its own beauty. Spring (March–May) for tulips, Summer (June–August) for green meadows, Autumn (September–November) for golden Chinar leaves, and Winter (December–February) for snow and skiing.
Do you provide airport pickup and drop?
Yes. Our service starts with airport pickup on arrival and ends with airport drop on departure. All transfers between airport, hotels, and sightseeing spots are included in our packages.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept both online and offline payments. Our travel agency name, account details, and payment options will be shared with you after booking confirmation.
Can we customize the package?
Yes, you can customize your package before final confirmation. Tell us your travel dates, number of travellers, and preferred destinations — we'll design an itinerary that fits your needs.

Day-wise Itinerary

Day 1: Arrive Srinagar

On arrival at Srinagar Airport (15 km), our representative will meet and greet you. Visit the famous Mughal Gardens — Cheshma Shahi (built by Emperor Shah Jahan), Nishat Bagh (Garden of Pleasure), and Shalimar Bagh (Garden of Love). Visit Shankaracharya Temple for a panoramic view of Srinagar city. In the evening, enjoy a 1-hour Shikara ride on Dal Lake. Dinner & overnight at Srinagar Hotel.

Day 2: Srinagar → Pahalgam

After breakfast, drive to Pahalgam (95 km / 2.5 hours), also known as the 'Valley of Shepherds,' via saffron fields and apple orchards. Visit Aru Valley (12 km), Chandanwari (16 km — the starting point of Amarnath Yatra), and Betaab Valley (famous from the Bollywood movie Betaab). Note: These points are accessed by local union taxi or pony at your own cost. Dinner & overnight at Pahalgam Hotel.

Day 3: Pahalgam → Srinagar Houseboat

After breakfast, return to Srinagar (95 km / 2.5 hours). Visit the local handicraft shops and the famous Lal Chowk. In the evening, check in to a traditional Houseboat on Dal Lake. Enjoy the serene views and a complimentary Shikara ride. Dinner & overnight at Houseboat.

Day 4: Srinagar Airport Drop

After breakfast, check out from the houseboat. Transfer to Srinagar Airport (15 km) for your onward journey. Depart with beautiful memories of Kashmir.
